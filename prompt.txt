"Research in personas has been prominent at HCI conferences since the early 2000s. The term has had an implied definition that it is a tool for design that represent a group of users. With AI the development of personas is discussed and terms such as synthetic personas and AI personas has appeared, but this is not the only issue that surfaces new fields are entering HCI with new understandings of the term personas, such as treating a SoMe persona. We report on a workshop on AI and personas at CHI2026 that resulted in misunderstandings, of the term personas. We analyze the papers submitted and find differences in the understanding of term personas. We reflect on whether we need to go back to some of the early vocabulary of groups of users for design reasons or if we need to add a prefix to the term such as design personas, user personas, NLP personas, self expression personas, etc."
